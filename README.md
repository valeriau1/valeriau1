## Research

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/EuPi8nC.png" alt="Cortex glia immunostaining" align="right" width="340">

**Glial regulation of seizures** in the *easPC80* epilepsy mutant

Using GAL4/UAS RNAi to knock down EAAT1 in astrocytes and cortex glia separately, then comparing seizure behavior and glial morphology. The goal is to figure out whether compartment-specific glutamate clearance drives or damps seizure activity.

`immunostaining` `confocal imaging` `behavioral assays` `RNAi` `GAL4/UAS`
